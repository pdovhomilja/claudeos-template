1. **Read** `wiki/goals.md`, `wiki/tasks.md`, the newest brief in `wiki/twin/` (if any), open rows in `wiki/topics/waiting-for.md`, and today's calendar if a calendar tool is connected.
2. **Propose the day**, in this exact shape, nothing else:
   - **Must today (1):** the task with the nearest hard date or the biggest goal gap per hour.
   - **Should (2):** the next two.
   - **Blocked on you:** decisions only (from the brief's "Needs you" and from tasks waiting on the user).
   - **Calendar:** meetings that cut the day, one line. No calendar tool → omit.
3. Wait for "ok" or a swap. On "ok": mark the Must in `wiki/tasks.md` with `(must YYYY-MM-DD)`. On a swap: do it, no debate.

Keep the whole reply under 15 lines. No motivational text.
